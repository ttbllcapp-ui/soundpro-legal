How do I restore my purchase?

Open Sound Pro, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for your original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID, not inside the App. On your iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions, select Sound Pro, and cancel there.

Why does Sound Pro need microphone access?

The Meter tab uses your microphone to measure ambient noise levels in real time. Audio is processed entirely on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

The volume boost sounds distorted at high levels

Volume boost amplifies your device's audio output beyond its normal limit, which can introduce distortion at very high boost levels or on small speakers. Try lowering the boost percentage or using headphones for cleaner sound.
